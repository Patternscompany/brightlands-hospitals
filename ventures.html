Brightlands Derma

The premium dermatology and aesthetic division of Brightlands, delivering personalised, result-orientated clinical care. Brightlands Derma combines evidence-based dermatology with the latest in aesthetic science to help patients achieve confidence through expert-led transformations.

  • Aesthetic Enhancements: Non-surgical and minimally invasive aesthetic procedures for natural, lasting results

UniRad Teleradiology

Unirad is Brightlands' advanced diagnostic and imaging division, delivering high-accuracy radiology reporting and interventional services. With internationally certified leadership and a technology-first infrastructure, Unirad is positioned to serve hospitals, clinics, and healthcare networks globally.

  • MRI & CT Scans: High-resolution imaging with expert interpretation for neurological, cardiac, musculoskeletal, and oncological assessment
  • Ultrasound Imaging: Comprehensive ultrasound services including obstetric, abdominal, vascular, and musculoskeletal applications
  • Teleradiology: Cloud-enabled remote radiology reporting services connecting international hospitals to specialist radiologists in real time
  • Interventional Radiology: Minimally invasive image-guided procedures for diagnosis and treatment with precision and reduced patient risk
